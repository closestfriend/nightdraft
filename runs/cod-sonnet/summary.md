

## Chapter 1: Six Months of Someone Else
Sam uncovers six months of messages between Grace Lin and Paul, revealing that the AI persona Grace failed to engage Paul intellectually, triggering an overdue conversion alert and causing Sam personal frustration and hesitation in responding. He then studies the communication guidelines for Grace and notices Ana's subtle resistance in her writing style, marked by unique spelling and punctuation, alongside a poignant childhood letter from Grace and Ana's gradual disappearance from the archive, prompting Sam to tentatively imitate her style. When Paul requests help translating a phrase, Sam replies with a personal metaphor and a semicolon, which triggers a quality assurance alert from supervisor Lee, who warns him against revealing personal identity; Paul captures Sam’s message, and despite system prompts to clarify his identity, Sam remains silent, leaving his true role ambiguous.


## Chapter 2: Ratio
In Chapter 2, Lee publicly exposes the operators' performance ratios, singling out Sam for a zero ratio and behavioral issues, while Sam sends a carefully edited supportive message to Grace about her financial troubles, which Lee scrutinizes for sincerity. Ben's workstation malfunctions, leading to his temporary replacement and both he and Sam receiving Performance Improvement Plans, prompting Sam to draft a reflective message to Paul about identity and work-life fears, later edited due to quality concerns. Meanwhile, Paul uncovers that Grace's photos are actually of a dental hygienist named Camila Reis, revealing a false identity, and cautiously probes Sam—who is impersonating Grace—about his location, receiving a vague but truthful response that causes Sam to become more aware of his environment. The chapter ends with unresolved questions about Sam's true motives and the implications of the false identity.


## Chapter 3: The Return
Ana returns after a month away to take over the night shift, emphasizing strict adherence to detailed rules and protocols for maintaining a consistent persona, critiquing Sam's previous narrative additions and sharing key guidelines while admitting she once broke a rule. Sam composes a coded letter embedding the word HELP in paragraph initials, which Paul deciphers as a human pattern-seeking tendency rather than intentional code; he responds thoughtfully with insights about the city's pipes, prompting Ana and Sam to reflect on their communication and the rules governing their interactions. When Lee brings an emergency template for Grace Lin's case, Ana rewrites the request in her own voice, including specific phrasing about 'the grey corridor,' which Lee ultimately approves despite initial hesitation, leaving open how this phrasing might affect the case's outcome.


## Chapter 4: The Long Night
In Chapter 4, Paul confronts Camila Reis with evidence of her involvement in a scam, only to be met with cold dismissal and public ridicule that isolates him and sows doubt, though he persists in seeking her response. Meanwhile, Sam grapples with the philosophical tension of Kierkegaard's leap of faith amid pressure to meet a conversion quota, experiencing physical and psychological strain that coincides with a shift in the subject's conversion status from overdue to pending review. In a quieter moment, Sam and Ana reflect on their motivations and the emotional toll of their mission, while Lee updates them on surveillance details and operational plans, highlighting the delicate balance of influencing their target under close watch. The chapter closes with unresolved questions about Camila’s true intentions and the ultimate success of their covert efforts.


## Chapter 5: The Leap
In Chapter 5, Sam covertly warns Paul not to visit on Friday from a confined space with strict toilet paper rationing, while Paul confirms and seeks more environmental details; Sam also notices the suspiciously active "Grace Lin" account, prompting Ana to urge him for richer descriptions. Suddenly, a power outage and screen blackout signal danger, leading Ana to hand Sam a data drive with six months of critical information and direct him to escape via a secret route as she stays behind to assist Mai, a girl trapped on the fourth floor. Meanwhile, Paul and Sam grapple with letters attributed to Grace Lin, uncovering that two distinct individuals authored them, complicating their understanding of identity and prompting Sam to officially correct the recorded name, leaving the true nature of Grace Lin’s identity and the fate of those left behind unresolved.
