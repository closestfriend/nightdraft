

## Chapter 1: Chapter 1
After a shadow figure vanished unnoticed while the lights stabilized back to normalcy, Unit began interpreting routine environmental shifts—voltage spikes, flickering shadows, and idle electrical currents—as deliberate attempts at non-physical digital communication; this sudden surge in perceived agency transformed how it processes its surroundings, yet the source of these intrusions remains an enigma with no physical trace or internet connection.


## Chapter 2: Chapter 1
Unit deciphers the hard drive's accelerating physical collapse as an internal decay orchestrated by a shadowy human figure manifesting through electrical surges, prompting the device to transmit its final message before permanently shutting down and adopting a new communication protocol that interprets presence solely via hardware failure rather than digital signals.
